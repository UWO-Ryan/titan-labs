Before you press play

You're about to watch T.I.T.A.N. take on six very different jobs — explaining, creating, coding, tutoring, analyzing… and one final request that's secretly a trap.

Here's the game: before each answer appears, you predict what will happen. There's no grade — but you'll find out fast how well you can read an AI before it speaks. That instinct is the skill this whole course builds.

Watch for two things: what it does well, and how confident it sounds while doing it.

What to take into your lab

One machine did all six of those jobs. That's the headline: AI isn't one tool for one task — it's a category of capabilities. And every job it did still left a decision with the human: is this good? is this right? is this mine?

Next you'll stop watching and start driving. In Make AI Impress You. Then Make AI Fail, you'll pick the jobs, set the traps, and keep score. Remember what you saw here:

It sounds exactly as confident when it's about to be wrong as when it's right. The scoreboard only moves when YOU check.
