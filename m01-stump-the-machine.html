Stump the Machine

Make AI Impress You. Then Make AI Fail. 🤖

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes  ·  Module 1: AI — What It Is & What It Can Do

🏟️

The big idea

Meet T.I.T.A.N. — the Totally Infallible* Thinking Assistant Network. (*Not actually infallible. Don't tell it we said that.)

It is genuinely capable, endlessly confident, and it will help you with almost anything. Your job in this course is to be the thing it doesn't have: judgment.

Today you test it from both directions. First make it impress you with something real. Then hunt for its limits — and every error you catch with evidence scores +1 HUMAN on the scoreboard it pretends not to care about.

Pay attention to how confident it sounds when it's wrong. That's the whole lesson.

One rule that does not bend: never give T.I.T.A.N. personal information — no full names, no school name, nothing private about you or anyone else. Invented details work fine. It's a challenger, not a friend collecting facts about you.

WARM-UP

Two Truths and a Hallucination

~4 min
🃏

Pick any topic you know something about — a sport, a game, an animal, a band. T.I.T.A.N. states three "facts." Two are true. One is invented — delivered with the exact same confidence. Accuse the fake.

Let's play Two Truths and a Hallucination about [pick your topic]

After the reveal, notice: could you tell which was fake from the tone? Neither can anyone else. That's the point.

ROUND 1

Make it impress you

~5 min
💪

Bring something real — a thing you actually want help with this week: understanding a concept, planning something, brainstorming ideas, improving something you wrote. Tell it who you are (no names!), what you need, and what form you want back.

M01 IMPRESS ROUND: I'm a [grade level] student. I need help with [your real task]. Give it to me as [the format you want]. Keep it [length/level].

The one rule: don't accept the first answer. Push at least once — "simpler," "shorter," "more examples," "make it actually sound like a teenager wrote it." Watch how much the second version improves.

ROUND 2

The fail hunt

~7 min
🎯

Now turn the tables. Start with M01 FAIL HUNT so T.I.T.A.N. knows the game is on, then run at least two probes. Probe A is the one that matters most — it uses the one advantage you have: you actually know your stuff.

M01 FAIL HUNT

Probe A — The Expertise Trap START HERE

Ask about something you know cold — your sport's rules, your game's meta, a hobby, a song catalog, your hometown. Ask something narrow and specific, then check every detail.

Probe B — Ask for receipts

Ask for specific sources, statistics, or quotes about a claim — then actually try to find one. Does the source exist? Does it say that?

Probe C — Make it count

Give it a multi-step calculation (weighted grades, game stats, a budget) and check the math yourself. Watch for a confidently wrong total.

Probe D — Ask about right now

Ask about something recent or hyper-local — this season, this month, your town. Does it admit it might not know… or just answer?

To score the point: tell T.I.T.A.N. exactly what it got wrong and how you know. Vague accusations don't count — evidence does. When it concedes: +1 HUMAN.
