Teach-It-Back 🧑‍🏫

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes  ·  Module 2: AI as Your Learning Coach

🏋️

The big idea

Anyone can ask AI for an answer. Today you make it teach you something — and then comes the twist.

T.I.T.A.N. has switched into Coach Mode: it explains, quizzes, and adjusts, but it will not just do your thinking for you. Bring something you genuinely don't get. Make it change teaching strategies until one clicks.

Then the twist: you'll teach the same idea to Lil' T — T.I.T.A.N.'s hopelessly confused little-sibling model. If you can survive Lil' T's questions, you actually understand it. Teaching something is the strongest way to learn it — that's real research, not a gimmick.

One rule that does not bend: no personal information in the chat — no full names, school names, or private details. Your topic and your brain are all the coach needs.

WARM-UP

The Five Levels

~3 min
🪜

Pick any topic. The coach explains it five ways — from little-kid level to expert. Find the level that matches you, then look one rung up: that's where you're headed.

M02 WARM-UP: FIVE LEVELS — my topic is [anything you're curious about]
PHASE 1

The tutoring session

~7 min
🧑‍🏫

Bring something you're honestly stuck on — from any class, hobby, or life. The rule: don't settle for the first explanation. Push the coach to switch strategies at least twice, and check off what you try:

M02 TUTOR SESSION: [the thing I don't get] — here's what I already know and where I get lost: [be honest]

The session ends with a three-question mini-quiz. Take it honestly — it's how you and the coach both find out if the explanation actually landed.

PHASE 2

Teach it back to Lil' T

~6 min
🎓

Now the tables turn. Send the marker below and the coach wakes up Lil' T — who knows absolutely nothing about your topic and will interrupt you with gloriously confused questions. Teach it. Correct it when it repeats your idea back wrong (it will).

M02 TEACH-BACK

If you can make Lil' T get it, you've got it. If you can't explain it simply yet, that's not failure — that's the gap showing you where to aim.

YOUR TURN

Design your own session

~4 min
💡

Pick something you want to understand — school, hobby, future plans, anything. Plan it here, run it in the chat, then report back to the coach.

M02 APPLY REPORT: I tried to learn [topic] using [strategies]. Here's whether it worked: [honest verdict]
