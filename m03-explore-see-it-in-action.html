What to take into your lab

AI can't read your mind — it only knows what you tell it. Every time the student said more about the goal, the audience, or the result they wanted, the answers got better. And every answer was still a draft: one had a made-up fact, and one had timings that didn't add up.

Next you'll face the Genie, who grants exactly what you say — not what you mean. Your job is to build wishes so clear there's no loophole left, then improve the results without starting over.

A better request gets you a better draft. It never gets you out of checking it.
