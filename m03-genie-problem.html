The Genie Problem

Fix the Prompt 🧞

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes  ·  Module 3: Getting Better Results from AI

🧞

The big idea

T.I.T.A.N. has been trapped in a lamp on the TitanLabs servers, and it is being extremely petty about it.

In Genie Mode, it grants exactly what you ask for. Ask for "help with your project," and you may receive ideas for building a moderately sized ziggurat. It's not broken. It's making a point:

AI gives you what you say — not what you mean.

Your job in this lab: learn to build wishes so clear the genie has no loopholes left. You'll use the framework from the Learn page:

GOAL→ CONTEXT→ DIRECTIONS→ CONSTRAINTS→ EVALUATE→ REFINE

One rule that does not bend: never give the genie personal information — no full names, no school name, no addresses, nothing private about you or anyone else. Invented details work fine ("a 9th grader who has a biology test Friday"). The genie doesn't need your name. It needs your clarity.

HOLE 1

The Study Wish

Par 3 · ~4 min
🌪️

Start with this deliberately terrible wish, exactly as written. Then rebuild it until the genie has no choice but to actually help. Swap in any subject you really study — the genie judges the build, not the subject.

HOLE 1 START: help me study

Your improved wish must clearly include:

  • GOAL The subject and what you're preparing for — and roughly when
  • CONTEXT Your level: what you already know, where you struggle
  • DIRECTIONS The form of help you want — plan, practice quiz, flashcards…
  • CONSTRAINTS At least one real limit — length, number of questions, time you have
0
HOLE 2

The Project Wish

Par 3 · ~4 min
🧺

This time the weak wish is missing directions and constraints — so the genie will bury you in gloriously useless ideas. Make it produce exactly what you can use.

HOLE 2 START: give me ideas for my project

Your improved wish must clearly include:

  • GOAL What the project actually is, and for what class or purpose
  • CONTEXT Audience or grade level, plus anything already decided
  • DIRECTIONS An exact number of ideas, in a format you name
  • CONSTRAINTS At least two real limits — time, materials, budget, rules
0
HOLE 3

The Polishing Wish

Par 4 · ~5 min
💎

This hole has a twist: no new wishes allowed. The genie will hand you the blandest paragraph about Wisconsin ever generated. You have to fix it by giving follow-up instructions — evaluate what's weak, tell the genie what to change, repeat. That's the skill that separates good AI users from everyone else: improving a result instead of starting over.

HOLE 3 START: write something about Wisconsin

Steer the draft — through at least two rounds of changes — until it has:

  • AUDIENCE A specific reader and purpose you chose
  • DETAILS Concrete, vivid specifics instead of generic filler
  • LENGTH A target length it actually hits
  • REFINE At least two follow-up instructions — evaluate, then improve
0
FINAL WISH

Free the genie

~4 min
✨

Now bring something real — a thing you actually want to learn, plan, create, or figure out. Build one wish with all four elements, look hard at what comes back, and refine it at least once. Do it well and the genie goes free… and tells you exactly what you've gotten good at.
