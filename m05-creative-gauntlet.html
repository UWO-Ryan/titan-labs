The Creative Gauntlet

Creative AI Lab 🎨

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes  ·  Module 5: Create with AI

🍳

The big idea

Cooking-show rules. You don't get to pick an easy project — the basket picks for you. A format, a subject, and one ridiculous twist.

Then the real rule of this lab: the first thing AI generates is never the final answer. You'll generate, look hard at it, tell the AI exactly what to change, and do that at least three times — with T.I.T.A.N.'s Creative Director critiquing between rounds (it has a beret; it has opinions).

Generating is the beginning of creating, not the end.

Two rules that do not bend: no personal information in any AI tool (your art needs no real names), and keep everything school-appropriate — including what you ask the image tool to make. If a generation comes out weird or inappropriate, don't share it — change your prompt and tell your instructor if it keeps happening.

STEP 3

Three rounds with the Director

~10 min
🎭

Between each generation, report to the Creative Director. Describe what you made (describing your own work is a skill — it forces you to actually look at it), get two critiques and a challenge, then go make Round 2. Three rounds minimum.

CRITIQUE ROUND 1: basket = [format / subject / twist]; what I made: [describe it honestly]; my prompt was: [paste your prompt]

You're allowed to reject the Director's notes — overruling AI feedback with a reason is creative direction, and the Director respects it.

Instructor setup needed: create the "T.I.T.A.N. — Creative Director" Playlab app (see personas/titan-director-m05.md) and paste its links into CONFIG in this file.
