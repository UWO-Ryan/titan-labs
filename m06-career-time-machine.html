The Career Time Machine 🕰️

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes  ·  Module 6: AI and Your Future

🕰️

The big idea

You're about to work the same job twice — once in 2026, once in 2036.

T.I.T.A.N.'s Time Machine will interview you about what you enjoy, propose careers (including one you've never heard of), and drop you into a day on the job. At every decision point you choose: hand this task to AI, or keep it human. Then you pull the lever and do the same day ten years from now.

One warning: along the way, the machine will state "facts" about pay, education, and job outlook, marked with 📌. It generates those the way it generates everything. Part of your mission is finding out which ones are actually true.

One rule that does not bend: share your interests, not your identity — no full names, school names, or private details. The Time Machine needs to know you like fixing things, not where you live.
