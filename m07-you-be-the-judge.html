The big idea

No new tool today. Today the tool is you.

Nine situations. For each, set the dial: Use it · It depends · Don't use it. After you rule, you'll see what's worth weighing — and how others called it. Spoiler: reasonable people disagree, and the why matters more than the ruling.

Then the hard part: take your toughest call to T.I.T.A.N.'s Devil's Advocate, who will argue the other side better than anyone you know. If your reasoning survives, it's real.

House rules: these scenarios stay hypothetical — don't share real personal stories with names or details in any AI chat. And your class or school's actual AI rules always outrank a lab verdict.
