What to take into your lab

From the first question to the last, the student decided: whether to use AI, which parts, what to verify and with whom, which draft to reject, and what still needed a human. The AI was useful the whole way — and still invented details nobody asked for.

Now it's your turn. In the Titan Games, you'll choose your own challenge, decide whether AI belongs in it, keep a build log, and defend your work to the Examiner.
