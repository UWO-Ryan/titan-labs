The big idea

Seven modules ago, you rubbed a lamp. Now nobody tells you what to type.

You choose a challenge that actually matters to you. You decide whether AI belongs in it, which tools fit, what to question, what to verify, and what stays yours. Then you face The Examiner — T.I.T.A.N.'s final form — and defend your work.

The first question is the one most people skip: should AI even be part of this? "No, not for that part" is a passing answer. It might be the best answer.

Standing rules, one last time: no personal information in AI tools, no paid subscriptions needed, school-appropriate everything — and if your challenge involves other people's info or feelings, Module 7 rides along.

STEP 1

Choose your quest

~5 min
🚩

Pick a pathway — or invent your own. The best challenge is one you'd want to do even if it weren't an assignment.

STEP 2

Build it — and keep the log

the real work
📓

Do the work with any approved tools you chose. As you go, log at least three moments — each one: what you did, what AI did, and what you decided. This log is your evidence for the Examiner.

👑

The final boss

Five questions. Real answers. Then the verdict.

STEP 3

Face the Examiner

~8 min
👑

Project done (or done enough to defend)? Then step into the arena. The Examiner asks five questions drawn from everything this course taught. Vague answers get sent back. Your build log is your ammunition.
